# Candidate Character Scope Audit

Use this when reviewing or synchronizing the `characters` field of candidate events.

1. Derive participants from the candidate’s trigger and observations, not from a chapter-wide cast list.
2. Include actors, direct speakers, recipients, and people whose explicitly described reaction is part of the event. Do not include everyone who appears elsewhere in the chapter.
3. A person mentioned only as context may be omitted; include them only when their reported action, decision, or relationship is material to the event.
4. Synchronize the focused list in the batch, progress data, normalized index and nested source record where those artifacts carry the field.

Completion criterion: each listed person maps to a concrete action, speech, receipt, or reaction in that candidate, and no unrelated chapter-only person remains.
